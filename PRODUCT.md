# El Nour Dashboard

## Product
Business management platform for Egyptian e-commerce stores. Manages orders (from EasyOrders), customers, products, and WhatsApp customer service with AI-powered auto-replies.

## Users
Store owners and their employees. Primary language: Arabic (Egyptian dialect). All UI is RTL.

## Core Workflows
1. Login → Dashboard overview
2. View/manage orders from EasyOrders
3. Manage customers
4. Manage products/inventory
5. WhatsApp inbox with AI auto-reply
6. Configure integrations (EasyOrders, WhatsApp, AI)
7. View reports and export data

## Design Direction
- Arabic-first RTL SaaS dashboard
- Emerald/teal accent on clean neutral palette
- Operate mode: scanability, consistency, precision
- Professional, trustworthy, modern
- Light/dark mode support
